About Curb Crew

One chore, off your list

Curb Crew is a neighborhood bin valet. Before your pickup day we roll your bins to the curb. After the truck has come through, we return them to the spot you picked, whether that is by the garage, behind the fence or inside the gate.

People sign up because they forget trash day, travel a lot, work early or late, have a steep driveway, or would rather not think about it. Whatever the reason, your bins end up where they should be and you never have to lift a finger.

It is a small convenience that adds up to a lot less stress.

FAQ

Good questions

Something missing? Send us a note below.

Do I need to be home?

No. The service is completely hands-off. As long as we can reach your bins, we take care of the rest.

Where will you return my bins?

Wherever you choose: beside the garage, behind the house, inside your gate or another agreed spot.

What if my bins are behind a gate or fence?

Tell us when you sign up. We will agree on the best way to get in so service runs smoothly each week.

What if my pickup schedule changes?

No problem. Let us know as soon as you can and we will update your schedule, including holiday shifts.

Can I pause my service?

Yes. For a vacation or any other reason, contact us and we will pause it, then restart when you are ready.

Is there a contract?

No. Most customers start month to month. The annual plan is there if you want the lower rate.

What about HOA rules?

Many HOAs require bins to leave the curb promptly after pickup. Returning them after collection helps keep you in compliance.

What areas do you serve?

Select neighborhoods in and around Maple Hollow. If you are nearby and do not see your street listed, ask us.

How do I get started?

Send the form below. We will confirm your address, pickup day and preferred bin spot before service begins.
